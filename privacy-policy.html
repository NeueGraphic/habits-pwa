Privacy Policy

Habits App  ·  Last updated: June 2025

🔒 Habits does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

Habits is a personal productivity app that helps you track your daily routines. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information — which, in short, is that we don't handle it at all.

Data We Collect

We collect no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any identifying information
  • We do not collect usage statistics or analytics
  • We do not collect crash reports or diagnostic data
  • We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs
  • We do not use advertising networks
  • We do not track your location

How Your Data is Stored

All data you create in Habits — your habit names, completion history, and streaks — is stored exclusively on your device using your device's local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service by us (it may be included in your iOS device backups managed by Apple)
  • Is permanently deleted when you delete the app

Internet Access

Habits does not require an internet connection to function. The app does not make any network requests.

Third-Party Services

Habits does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

Habits does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no registration, no accounts, and no data collection of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices (for example, if we add cloud sync features in the future), we will update this policy and the date at the top. Any new features that involve data handling will be opt-in and clearly disclosed.

Your Rights

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to request, export, or delete. Your data lives entirely on your device and is fully under your control.
